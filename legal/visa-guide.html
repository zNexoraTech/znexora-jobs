Which jobs can I do with my visa?

Your status of residence (在留資格) decides what work you can do in Japan. Check the type on the front of your residence card, and the work permission on the back.

For Myanmar nationals

Since 2021, Japan's emergency measure lets many Myanmar people stay and work with the status Designated Activities (特定活動). Some designations allow full-time work, others only part-time. Rules and screening have changed over time, so always check your designation paper (指定書) and the Immigration Services Agency's latest notice.

    For employers

    Before hiring, check the applicant's residence card in person: the status, the period of stay, and the work permission on the back. You can check whether a card number is valid on the Immigration Services Agency website. Hiring someone outside their permitted work is illegal for the employer too.
