How much can I work on my visa?

What work you can do depends on your status of residence (在留資格).

  • Students (留学): you need permission to work (資格外活動許可), usually written on your residence card. The limit is generally 28 hours a week, and up to 8 hours a day during long school holidays.
  • Dependants (家族滞在): also need permission, with similar limits.
  • Work visas (e.g. Engineer/Specialist in Humanities, Specified Skilled Worker): only the type of work your visa allows.
  • Permanent residents, spouses of Japanese nationals, long-term residents: no work restrictions.

If you're unsure, ask the Immigration Services Agency or FRESC (links below) before you accept a job.

Writing a Japanese CV (rirekisho)

  • Include: name, contact details, education, work history, Japanese level (JLPT), licences, and why you want the job.
  • A clear photo is common in Japan, but not required on this site.
  • Leave out: residence card number, passport number, Myanmar NRC number, family details. Employers check documents in person when hiring.
  • Save it as a PDF before uploading.

Never pay to get a job

No employer on this site may ask you for money: no "registration fee", "training deposit" or "visa fee". If anyone asks, don't pay. Use Report this job on the job page and we'll investigate.

Be careful of jobs that say "no visa check" or "cash only". Working outside your visa conditions can cost you your status of residence.

Your rights at work

  • You must be paid at least the minimum wage for your prefecture, whatever your nationality.
  • Ask for your working conditions in writing (労働条件通知書) before you start.
  • Overtime, night work and holiday work are paid at higher rates.
  • If you're treated unfairly, free help is available in many languages.
