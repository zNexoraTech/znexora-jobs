Guide for job seekers

  • Check your work limits. What work you can do depends on your status of residence. For example, students usually need permission for part-time work and are generally limited to 28 hours a week.
  • Never pay to get a job. No real employer on this site will ask you for money. Report it if anyone does.
  • Get your working conditions in writing (労働条件通知書) before you start.
  • Protect your ID. Don't put your residence card, passport or Myanmar ID number in your CV or messages.
