Privacy Policy (DRAFT)

DRAFT — have this reviewed before public launch (Act on the Protection of Personal Information).

What we collect

  • Account: email, name, language, role.
  • Job seekers: prefecture, Japanese level, skills, job preferences, optional visa type, application messages and CV files you upload.
  • Employers: company name, corporate number, address, phone, website.

We do not ask for passport, residence card or Myanmar ID numbers. Please don't upload them.

Why we use it

To run your account, show your application to the employer you apply to, prevent fraud, and contact you about the service.

Who can see it

Your application and CV are visible only to the employer of the job you apply to, and to our administrators for safety checks. We don't sell personal data.

Where it's stored

Supabase (database and file storage, Tokyo region) and Cloudflare (website hosting).

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time from your dashboard; this removes your profile, applications and CV files.
