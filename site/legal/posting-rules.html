Posting rules

Every job is checked before it goes live. We will reject or remove jobs that:

  • Offer work without checking residence status ("no visa check", "cash only").
  • Pay less than the minimum wage of the prefecture.
  • Ask applicants for any fee, deposit or payment.
  • Discriminate by nationality, religion, ethnicity, gender or age (except where legally allowed).
  • Are in the adult entertainment industry, or are vague "earn fast" offers.
  • Recruit for work outside Japan, or are posted by an unverified third party.
  • Contain false or misleading information about pay, hours or the employer.
